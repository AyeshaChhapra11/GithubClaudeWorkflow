FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I own the videos I create?

Yes. On any paid plan you hold full commercial rights to everything you generate with Moviant AI.

How long does a render take?

Most clips render in under 60 seconds. Longer scenes and 4K exports scale with length and complexity.

Can I use my own footage or assets?

Absolutely. Upload reference images, brand kits, and voice samples to guide the model's output.

Is there an API?

Yes — Enterprise plans include programmatic access so you can generate video directly from your apps.
